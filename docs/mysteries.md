# Open questions

Fifteen questions the code can settle. Each one closes with an entry in [discoveries.md](discoveries.md) that cites its function and address. Status of what the community already knows: [community-knowledge.md](community-knowledge.md).

## Graph and dream start

- [x] 1. Is the "last event" term of each graph axis `last / 3` (lsddecomp's reading) or the community's five-step table? They only differ at plus or minus 9. Answered: `last / 3`, checked in the emulator ([how a day becomes a point](discoveries.md#-how-a-day-becomes-a-point-on-the-graph)).
- [x] 2. How does `GetStageChunkFromMood` pick the starting chunk? What does the sentinel `{9,0}` mean, and why do some results land outside the graph? Answered, in the code and data: the first area whose mood equals yesterday's point, else place 1; `{9,0}` marks 106 empty filler squares, and since it is also a real graph point it sends you to place 1; 93 points name a square with no arrival point, so you wake up on another square of the right place ([how the graph picks where tomorrow starts](discoveries.md#-how-the-graph-picks-where-tomorrow-starts)). Whether a filler square can be walked into was not checked.
- [x] 3. What is the `SPECIAL_COLORS` table for? Answered: it names the day colour that 8 spawn rules need to bring out extra characters, nothing to do with links ([the spawn rules](discoveries.md#-the-hidden-rules-that-decide-which-characters-appear)); several are checked in the game, such as the red-day crowd of maze penguins in Happy Town ([the penguins](discoveries.md#-a-red-day-fills-the-maze-of-questions-with-rainbow-penguins)) and the green-day Buddha statues of Kyoto ([the statues](discoveries.md#-a-green-day-sends-a-file-of-buddha-statues-through-kyoto)).
- [x] 4. What triggers the "video reel", when four graph cells blink green? Answered: your last 100 days must include the four cells (-1, 1), (1, 1), (0, 0) and (0, -3), once per game; the film is 20 special-day videos back to back. Checked in the emulator ([the video reel](discoveries.md#-the-secret-video-reel-four-graph-points-and-11-minutes-of-film)).

## Grey Man and Flashback

- [x] 5. Grey Man spawn logic: day parity, odds per zone, timers. Does he always erase the flashbacks? Answered: even days, 1 in 12 on a spot left empty, never in six places, he leaves after a fixed time, and every touch erases them ([when](discoveries.md#-when-the-grey-man-can-appear), [where](discoveries.md#-who-can-meet-the-grey-man-and-where), [what he does](discoveries.md#-the-grey-man-flips-your-graph-and-wipes-your-memories)).
- [x] 6. Which total score unlocks Flashback? What are the 30 "navigation challenges", and how is the "instance" score earned? Answered: above 9,999,999 ([the unlock](discoveries.md#-what-unlocks-flashback-in-the-menu)); the 30 goals are the fixed links, one unreachable ([the goals](discoveries.md#-fixed-links-are-the-navigation-goals-and-one-can-never-be-completed)); the instance score comes from touching characters ([the records](discoveries.md#-the-character-record-mood-points-waking-up-and-leaving)).

## Days, textures, randomness

- [x] 7. The full list of the 42 special days and their videos. Why do players see 2 variants when the code seems to draw from 6? Answered: [the 42 days](discoveries.md#-the-42-special-days); 2 videos and 4 still pictures per category ([what a special day shows](discoveries.md#-what-a-special-day-shows)).
- [x] 8. What happens on day 365, then from year 1 on? Does the year counter change anything? Answered: the ending plays when the year closes ([the ending](discoveries.md#-the-ending-plays-when-a-year-closes-not-on-day-365)); the year only changes the first dream of the next year, through a bug ([the wrong data](discoveries.md#-the-first-dream-of-a-new-year-starts-from-the-wrong-data)).
- [x] 9. How is the texture set chosen (a 10/20/30/40-day cycle?), and what is `TEXD` really? Answered: a 40-day cycle, set D is one more random texture set ([the cycle](discoveries.md#-the-40-day-texture-cycle)).
- [ ] 10. What is the seed "World: a b c"? How is `rand()` seeded? Where do the A/B day patterns come from? Partly answered ([the dice](discoveries.md#-the-dice-of-the-game)): `rand()` is almost never reseeded, and the A/B days are odd and even day numbers. The word "world" is nowhere on the disc, so that picture comes from another build or a tool; what its three numbers were is unknown.
- [ ] 11. What causes the glitch textures, and why does the timer grow at every link in that state? Partly answered ([glitch textures](discoveries.md#-glitch-textures-a-texture-set-that-failed-to-load)): a texture set that fails to load for lack of memory, and each failing place sets the dream to end 30 seconds later, checked in the emulator. A texture set needs one block of 198,656 bytes with only 17 to 23 KB to spare; zeroing "location_time" (0x8008AC70) re-runs the place's first-tick setup and leaks its scenery objects, and one such leak makes every later dream fail until power-off, checked in the emulator. What leaks in natural play is not found: 5 dreams and one long dream with 74 links leaked nothing.

## Events and hidden content

- [ ] 12. Exact conditions of each rare event: flying train, astronaut bar, special lions, multiple gunmen and minotaurs. Mostly answered, each sight seen in the emulator ([Rare sights](discoveries.md#rare-sights)): the flying train is Happy Town's odd-day train in a dream long enough for it to reach the opera pillar, which a dream started in Happy Town never is; waking it makes the day pink by itself, played with only the day and yesterday's point forced ([the train](discoveries.md#-the-flying-train-a-pink-day-and-a-long-dream)); the astronaut lands at the bar on an even pink day, reached from a Happy Town start with a wall link 7 times in 48 (played with that link's dice forced), and "days 200 to 300" is a myth ([the astronaut](discoveries.md#-the-astronaut-lands-at-the-bar-on-a-pink-day)); the minotaurs are three Kyoto rickshaws on an odd yellow day ([the minotaurs](discoveries.md#-three-minotaur-rickshaws-on-a-yellow-day-in-kyoto)). The figures we first took for the "multiple gunmen" are the rainbow penguins of the Maze of Questions and their red-day crowd ([the penguins](discoveries.md#-a-red-day-fills-the-maze-of-questions-with-rainbow-penguins)). The real gunman is Violence District's type 85, played in the emulator with and without CIRCLE; three stand on one square every even day, and on an even cyan day four more stand next to them: our best match for the sighting, by type and spawn log, not seen on screen ([the CIRCLE characters](discoveries.md#-characters-that-answer-to-circle)). Other colour sights found since: [Buddha statues](discoveries.md#-a-green-day-sends-a-file-of-buddha-statues-through-kyoto), [red-headed runners](discoveries.md#-a-white-day-brings-two-more-red-headed-runners-to-a-kyoto-plaza), [the Black Space giant](discoveries.md#-a-yellow-day-gives-black-spaces-walker-a-giant-twin), [the fetuses](discoveries.md#-a-blue-day-sends-four-more-fetuses-down-the-flesh-tunnels), and size rules ([flowers and mushrooms](discoveries.md#-happy-towns-flowers-and-mushrooms-grow-with-the-day-number), [tiny lips](discoveries.md#-tiny-lips-walk-toward-you-in-happy-town)), and the four characters that stretch or flatten a whole place, all seen in the emulator ([stretch](discoveries.md#-four-characters-stretch-or-flatten-the-whole-place)). Still open: the cyan-day gunmen on screen, the special lions (matched only by look and behaviour to Pit & Temple's yellow-day trio, [the purple beasts](discoveries.md#-three-purple-beasts-on-a-yellow-day-our-best-match-for-the-special-lions)), the Natural World lions (not searched), and a route to the train walked by pad through Happy Town.
- [x] 13. Do the static link variations per tile depend on the day or on the number of visits? Answered: neither; some extra links depend on which characters appeared ([fixed links](discoveries.md#-fixed-links-do-not-change-with-visits-but-some-depend-on-who-showed-up)); the timer-dependent pick of stage 12 belongs to a fall off an edge, and the emulator shows that pick is never used: a fall ends the dream like the clock ([falls and walls](discoveries.md#-falling-off-an-edge-walls-and-the-cottages-walking-squares)).
- [x] 14. Is there reachable code for the unused dog, the WC room or a debug menu? Is `ENDING.STR` referenced anywhere but day 365? Answered: [the dog](discoveries.md#-the-dog-that-never-loads) never loads; no WC room file and no debug menu in what was read ([never asked for](discoveries.md#-what-the-game-never-asks-for)), and no cheat input besides [the erase combo](discoveries.md#-select--triangle-in-the-pause-erases-your-game); `ENDING.STR` plays only when a year closes ([the ending](discoveries.md#-the-ending-plays-when-a-year-closes-not-on-day-365)).
- [ ] 15. The memory card save format, and the fields of `DreamSys` still unknown. Partly answered: [the save block](discoveries.md#-everything-the-game-remembers-fits-in-one-block) and [the save](discoveries.md#-how-the-memory-card-save-works); since then +0x5DF (the video reel flag), +0x678 (the SHAKE option) and +0x878 (never saved or loaded) are explained ([the save block](discoveries.md#-everything-the-game-remembers-fits-in-one-block)). The card driver and the fields +0x5D8, +0x67C, +0x680 and +0x684 are still unread.
