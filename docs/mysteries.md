# Open questions

The fifteen questions about how the game works that this project set out to answer from the code: 13 are answered and 2 (11 and 12) stay partly open. Each answer links to an entry in [discoveries.md](discoveries.md) that cites its function and address. Status of what the community already knows: [community-knowledge.md](community-knowledge.md).

## Graph and dream start

- [x] 1. Does the graph add a third of the last thing you met (`last / 3`, lsddecomp's reading) or follow the community's five-step table? They only differ at plus or minus 9. Answered: a third, checked in the emulator ([how a day becomes a point](discoveries.md#-how-a-day-becomes-a-point-on-the-graph)).
- [x] 2. How does the game pick the square a dream starts on (`GetStageChunkFromMood`)? What does the mood `{9,0}` mean, and why do some results land outside the graph? Answered, in the code and data: the first area whose mood equals yesterday's point, else place 1; `{9,0}` marks 106 empty filler squares, and since it is also a real graph point it sends you to place 1; 93 points name a square with no arrival point, so you wake up on another square of the right place ([how the graph picks where tomorrow starts](discoveries.md#-how-the-graph-picks-where-tomorrow-starts)). Whether a filler square can be walked into was not checked.
- [x] 3. What is the `SPECIAL_COLORS` table for? Answered: it names the day colour that 8 spawn rules need to bring out extra characters, nothing to do with links ([the spawn rules](discoveries.md#-the-hidden-rules-that-decide-which-characters-appear)); several are checked in the game, such as the red-day crowd of maze penguins in Happy Town ([the penguins](discoveries.md#-a-red-day-fills-the-maze-of-questions-with-rainbow-penguins)) and the green-day Buddha statues of Kyoto ([the statues](discoveries.md#-a-green-day-sends-a-file-of-buddha-statues-through-kyoto)).
- [x] 4. What triggers the "video reel", when four graph cells blink green? Answered: your last 100 days must include the four cells (-1, 1), (1, 1), (0, 0) and (0, -3), once per game; the film is 20 special-day videos back to back. Checked in the emulator ([the video reel](discoveries.md#-the-secret-video-reel-four-graph-points-and-11-minutes-of-film)).

## Grey Man and Flashback

- [x] 5. Grey Man spawn rules: odd or even days, odds per place, timers. Does he always erase the flashbacks? Answered: even days, 1 in 12 on a spot left empty, never in seven places (Sun Faces Heave among them, checked in the emulator with his die forced), he leaves after a fixed time, he is placed about four tiles in front of you, and every touch erases them ([when](discoveries.md#-when-the-grey-man-can-appear), [where](discoveries.md#-who-can-meet-the-grey-man-and-where), [what he does](discoveries.md#-the-grey-man-flips-your-graph-and-wipes-your-memories)).
- [x] 6. Which total score unlocks Flashback? What are the 30 "navigation challenges", and how is the "instance" score earned? Answered: above 9,999,999 ([the unlock](discoveries.md#-what-unlocks-flashback-in-the-menu)); the 30 goals are the fixed links, one unreachable ([the goals](discoveries.md#-fixed-links-are-the-navigation-goals-and-one-can-never-be-completed)); the instance score comes from touching characters ([the records](discoveries.md#-the-character-record-mood-points-waking-up-and-leaving)).

## Days, textures, randomness

- [x] 7. The full list of the 42 special days and their videos. Why do players see 2 variants when the code seems to draw from 6? Answered: [the 42 days](discoveries.md#-the-42-special-days); 2 videos and 4 still pictures per category ([what a special day shows](discoveries.md#-what-a-special-day-shows)).
- [x] 8. What happens on day 365, then from year 1 on? Does the year counter change anything? Answered: the ending plays when the year closes ([the ending](discoveries.md#-the-ending-plays-when-a-year-closes-not-on-day-365)); the year only changes the first dream of the next year, through a bug ([the wrong data](discoveries.md#-the-first-dream-of-a-new-year-starts-from-the-wrong-data)).
- [x] 9. How is the texture set chosen (a 10/20/30/40-day cycle?), and what is `TEXD` really? Answered: a 40-day cycle, set D is one more random texture set ([the cycle](discoveries.md#-the-40-day-texture-cycle)).
- [x] 10. What are the numbers in "World: a b c"? Where does the dice sequence (`rand()`) start? Where do the A/B day patterns come from? Answered ([the dice](discoveries.md#-the-dice-of-the-game)): `rand()` almost never gets a new start, and the A/B days are odd and even day numbers. The "World" picture is a 1998 screenshot of the developers' tools and its three numbers are the position (x, height, z) of the object on screen, repeated on the "Mtx T" line; nothing on the disc can draw it (no such text, no `FntPrint`, and Sony's only three-number printers, `print_vector` @0x80012BEC and `print_matrix` @0x80012B7C, are never called). What memory editors now call the "world seed" is `g_randState` @0x8008AC68 shown as two halves. Which tool drew the picture, and which object it shows, is not in the game's code and stays unknown.
- [ ] 11. What causes the glitch textures, and why does the timer grow at every link in that state? Partly answered ([glitch textures](discoveries.md#-glitch-textures-a-texture-set-that-failed-to-load)): a texture set that fails to load for lack of memory, and each failing place sets the dream to end 30 seconds later, checked in the emulator. A texture set needs one block of 198,656 bytes with only 17 to 23 KB to spare; setting "location_time" (0x8008AC70) to 0 rebuilds the place's scenery without freeing the old one, and one such loss makes every later dream fail until power-off, checked in the emulator. What loses memory in natural play is not found: the margin is the same for every stage, set and day (about 23 KB at the first place of a dream), and every ordinary path tested lost none, the menu coming back with the same 414 memory blocks after pause, save, load, the opening video, the graph, a full-length dream, 30 day changes with six special days, the flashback, the Grey Man, a fall, the gunman's shot, nine links on foot, the secret reel to its end and the year end, checked in the emulator. Not tried: dreams starting in stages 6 to 13, the cottage's scripted walks, the Ferris wheel's ending, the erase combo, memory card errors, a load of a save that has seen the reel, real CD timing, and long sessions with many links across days (about 200,000 vsyncs, close to an hour at real time, for 60 links). Still open: whether any natural path loses memory. Our code reading has not identified a natural path that loses memory. The longer sessions, card-error cases and timing conditions listed above remain untested.

## Events and hidden content

- [ ] 12. Exact conditions of each rare event: flying train, astronaut bar, special lions, multiple gunmen and minotaurs. Mostly answered, each sight seen in the emulator ([Rare sights](discoveries.md#rare-sights)): the flying train is Happy Town's odd-day train in a dream long enough for it to reach the opera pillar, which a dream started in Happy Town never is; waking it makes the day pink by itself, played with only the day and yesterday's point forced ([the train](discoveries.md#-the-flying-train-a-pink-day-and-a-long-dream)); the astronaut lands at the bar on an even pink day, reached from a Happy Town start with a wall link 7 times in 48 (played with that link's dice forced), and "days 200 to 300" is a myth ([the astronaut](discoveries.md#-the-astronaut-lands-at-the-bar-on-a-pink-day)); the minotaurs are three Kyoto rickshaws on an odd yellow day ([the minotaurs](discoveries.md#-three-minotaur-rickshaws-on-a-yellow-day-in-kyoto)). The figures we first took for the "multiple gunmen" are the rainbow penguins of the Maze of Questions and their red-day crowd ([the penguins](discoveries.md#-a-red-day-fills-the-maze-of-questions-with-rainbow-penguins)). The real gunman is Violence District's type 85, played in the emulator with and without CIRCLE; three stand on one square every even day, and on an even cyan day a row of four more stands on the quay behind the start, seen on screen with the colour forced (one hides inside a crane's base, so three at most are visible) and shooting you into Clockwork Machines: our best match for the sighting ([the CIRCLE characters](discoveries.md#-characters-that-answer-to-circle)). The special lions: our best match is now the Natural World's cyan-day pair of lions, the only lions on one day colour and the only ones that send you to Temple Dojo, seen in the emulator with the colour forced ([the lions](discoveries.md#-the-natural-world-lions-a-random-temper-and-a-cyan-day-pair-that-leads-to-temple-dojo)); Pit & Temple's yellow-day purple beasts, our first match, do not look like lions ([the purple beasts](discoveries.md#-three-purple-beasts-on-a-yellow-day-in-pit--temple)). The Natural World's two everyday lions draw a temper each, calm 2 times in 3; the charge was filmed: the lion grows into a giant, runs over the camera and its touch ends the dream. The cyan pair now comes without forcing the colour: a dream started on square (12, 5), 15 border crossings in that row, then a walk down to (9, 12) makes the day cyan on the lions' square, because a square's characters are made when you step next to it; played by moving the dreamer square to square, with only the day and yesterday's point written in. Walked with the pad alone, with the same two values written, the route logs its first 18 squares as planned, then the straight line out of square (10, 7) falls off a cliff and ends the dream. The horned creature by the cliff is the Abyss Demon, not a lion ([the Abyss Demon](discoveries.md#-the-abyss-demon-rises-at-the-cliff-edge-and-sends-you-to-kyoto)). Other colour sights found since: [Buddha statues](discoveries.md#-a-green-day-sends-a-file-of-buddha-statues-through-kyoto), [red-headed runners](discoveries.md#-a-white-day-brings-two-more-red-headed-runners-to-a-kyoto-plaza), [the Black Space giant](discoveries.md#-a-yellow-day-gives-black-spaces-walker-a-giant-twin), [the fetuses](discoveries.md#-a-blue-day-sends-four-more-fetuses-down-the-flesh-tunnels), and size rules ([flowers and mushrooms](discoveries.md#-happy-towns-flowers-and-mushrooms-grow-with-the-day-number), [tiny lips](discoveries.md#-tiny-lips-walk-toward-you-in-happy-town)), and the four characters that stretch or flatten a whole place, all seen in the emulator ([stretch](discoveries.md#-four-characters-stretch-or-flatten-the-whole-place)). The cyan gunmen's shot can drop you on a Clockwork Machines arrival point where the first step ends the dream, seen in the emulator. Still open: whether a way round the cliff of square (10, 7) reaches the cyan lions on foot (only the straight line was tried, and a detour through another square changes the logged sum, so the route search has to be redone with the unwalkable edges taken out), a walk that makes the day cyan on the gunmen's square (worked out on paper, not played), routes to the train and to the astronaut walked by pad through Happy Town (not tried), and whether "special lions near Temple Dojo" really means the cyan-day lions. The remaining routes need checks of the map geometry and movement rules, followed by a pad-only test. The community name "special lions" cannot by itself identify which lions were meant.
- [x] 13. Do a tile's fixed links change with the day or the number of visits? Answered: neither; some extra links depend on which characters appeared ([fixed links](discoveries.md#-fixed-links-do-not-change-with-visits-but-some-depend-on-who-showed-up)); the timer-dependent pick of stage 12 belongs to a fall off an edge, and the emulator shows that pick is never used: a fall ends the dream like the clock ([falls and walls](discoveries.md#-falling-off-an-edge-walls-and-the-cottages-walking-squares)). Only blocks marked as walls on the map can link you ([blocks and walls](discoveries.md#-the-world-is-built-from-square-blocks-and-only-marked-blocks-are-walls)), and the Long Hallway's room links you 2 times in 3 by a die roll ([the Long Hallway](discoveries.md#-the-long-hallways-room-turns-the-sky-blue-then-pulls-you-out-2-times-in-3)).
- [x] 14. Is there reachable code for the unused dog, the WC room or a debug menu? Is `ENDING.STR` referenced anywhere but day 365? Answered: [the dog](discoveries.md#-the-dog-that-never-loads) never loads; no WC room file, and no debug menu or on-screen debug text in the game code, every function of which has been read ([never asked for](discoveries.md#-what-the-game-never-asks-for)), and no cheat input besides [the erase combo](discoveries.md#-select--triangle-in-the-pause-erases-your-game); a few switches the shipped game leaves off are listed in [switches and features](discoveries.md#-switches-and-features-the-game-never-uses); `ENDING.STR` plays only when a year closes ([the ending](discoveries.md#-the-ending-plays-when-a-year-closes-not-on-day-365)).
- [x] 15. The memory card save format, and the fields of `DreamSys` still unknown. Answered: [the save block](discoveries.md#-everything-the-game-remembers-fits-in-one-block) and [the save](discoveries.md#-how-the-memory-card-save-works): the file is a 0x200-byte header ("SC", title, a three-frame icon) and the 1,792-byte block, one card block (`Card_WriteFileOnce` @0x8004EF6C); +0x5DF is the video reel flag, +0x678 the SHAKE option and +0x878 (never saved or loaded) the "never saved" flag. The fields +0x5D8, +0x67C, +0x680 and +0x684 (save block +0x460, +0x504, +0x508 and the 500 bytes at +0x50C) are spare room: a new game sets them to 0 (`DreamSys_InitNewGame` @0x8005A258), no other instruction of the game reads or writes them, and they only travel with the whole 1,792-byte block to the card and back. The 500 bytes end the block exactly on a memory card frame (14 frames of 128 bytes). The two words sit right after the SHAKE option and look like room for two more options; what they were meant for is not in the code, since nothing uses them. 122 more bytes of the block (+0x461 to +0x466, +0x48C to +0x4FF) are touched by nothing at all, not even a new game. Read in the code only. Where the save comment goes is known ([the comment keyboard](discoveries.md#-select-on-the-comment-keyboard-makes-the-cursor-run)).
